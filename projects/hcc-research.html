Recombinant Antigens for Early HCC Detection

Research on recombinant antigen generation for antibody production toward early detection of hepatocellular carcinoma.

Project title

Generation of Recombinant Antigens for Antibody Production toward Early Detection of Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC)

Research experience

Working under Dr. Vuong Thi Huyen Trang, I gained practical exposure to molecular biology workflows, including primer design, PCR amplification, vector transformation and screening using Sanger sequencing. I studied E. coli recombinant-protein expression, affinity chromatography, SDS-PAGE, Western blot, animal immunization protocols, indirect ELISA and ROC-based data analysis.

Research status

Research is ongoing, with a manuscript in preparation.
